decision: A send typed at the desk is answered on the trusted card, which shows the send row's code-made call (where it goes and the text's SHA-256 prefix); the full text shows in the Kai pane outside the card, drawn only when its own hash matches the card's, and Allow stays disabled until that text is drawn
---
Until now a send (`net_send`, up to 4 KB of text) typed at the desk was refused there and asked only on the console. Now the card shows the send row's call as the console's card does (#989), `net_send("host", "/path", "<16 hex>")`, the last argument the first 16 hex digits of the text's SHA-256; the `ONCE` names that call, and the shell sends only a text with that digest. `desktop` shows the full text in the Kai pane as a send preview, under a code-drawn header in the trusted layer's style, every character kept and anything outside printable ASCII escaped. It draws the text only when its own SHA-256 of the bytes begins with the digest in the kernel's posted call, so the shell carrying the text cannot make it differ unseen. The card takes no Allow until the preview for that ask is drawn. After Allow the send is held 5 s with undo, as on the console. Agents' sends are not part of this. Design: lane C's note of 2026-11-18, adapted to the send row's digest call. The amendment is ADR 0005 §7, "Amended 2026-11-19".
