decision: A send typed at the desk is answered on the trusted card, which shows a code-made digest of it (where it goes, how much, the text's SHA-256 prefix); the full text shows in the Kai pane outside the card, and Allow stays disabled until that text is drawn
---
Until now a send (`net_send`, up to 4 KB of text) typed at the desk was refused there, since a card's call line cannot show the text, and was asked only on the console. Now the kernel's terminal makes a digest of the call from the full call it holds, `net_send("host", "/path", text <n> B #<8 hex>)`, and the card shows that, never model or agent text. The `ONCE` digest still names the full call. `desktop` draws the full text in the Kai pane as a send preview, from the kernel's posted bytes, under a code-drawn header in the trusted layer's style, every character kept and anything outside printable ASCII escaped. The card's settle gate opens only after `desktop` tells the kernel the preview for that ask is drawn. After Allow the send is held 5 s with undo, as on the console. Only the long-text form comes to the desk; agents' sends are not part of this. Design: lane C's note of 2026-11-18. The amendment is ADR 0005 §7, "Amended 2026-11-19".
