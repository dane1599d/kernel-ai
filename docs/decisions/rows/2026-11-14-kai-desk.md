needed-by: P3 follow-up, the founder's redesign: PR 1 of 10 of the Kai Desk plan, decided 2026-11-14 (the founder: "Start redesign now"); the amendment's details need the founder's OK before the window chrome (PR 5) merges (AP-1; the manager reviews)
decision: ADR 0005 is amended: the desktop becomes "Kai Desk", a Windows 11-style desktop with a taskbar, a menu and a pane for Kai, in place of the "Ember Workbench" look and the `kai>` bar along the bottom
---
The founder asked for a desktop that looks and works like Windows 11, with a Kai twist, and said "Start redesign now". ADR 0005 is amended to say what that means (its status line is "Amended 2026-11-14: Kai Desk"; the changes are the amendment section at its top, §1 and the Decision paragraph, §7, §8, a note in §9, one row of the cost table and the open questions). In plain words, a person will see this. The top bar and the `kai>` bar along the bottom are gone. A taskbar runs along the bottom: the Kai button and the apps' icons in the middle, and at the right a Kai chip (ready, thinking, done), the memory in use and the time. Kai has a pane down the right edge with the input, the state line and the history of your lines and Kai's answers; it is closed when the desktop starts, and Ctrl+Space, the screen's corner or the chip opens it. A Kai menu opens with the Super key, Ctrl+Esc or the Kai button: one box that says "Ask Kai or search apps", your pinned apps, your recent lines and all apps; typing "notes" and pressing Enter opens Notes, and any other text asks Kai. Windows get rounder corners, wide flat minimize, maximize and close buttons, and titles that show the system's name for the app first and the app's own title after it, which is the rule ADR 0005 §7 always had and the code did not follow. The look is graphite in layers with ember kept for Kai alone, and the light and high-contrast themes follow. What does not change is the safety: the ask card is drawn last, over everything, and takes only y, n or a click; the state line stays code's alone; Kai's words show only in the pane's history, in Kai's own look, never in the system's; there is no blur or translucency, no motion is added, and nothing is Microsoft's (no Segoe, no Fluent shapes, no Microsoft blue, and the words "Start" and "Copilot" never appear in the UI). The founder is asked to confirm four calls, each the default the amendment builds. One: the pane floats over windows and is closed at boot, with no docking (open question 9). Two: the clock shows UTC, written on its date line, until a time-zone setting exists (open question 7). Three: the fonts stay Inter and JetBrains Mono, both free to use and neither Microsoft's, and a typeface drawn for Kai is a separate, large job (open question 6). Four: Super opens the menu and Ctrl+Space the pane, though whether Super reaches the guest in VirtualBox is unchecked, and the Kai button, the `apps` line, Ctrl+Space and the corner always work (open question 1). This pull request is the first of ten and moves no pixel: it is the amendment, this row, the p3 plan's pointers, and in the `look` crate the two new tokens `shell` (the taskbar, the pane and the menu's footer, held to the full contrast gate like `window`) and `hover` (hover and pressed fills, held to a gate only for what is drawn on it) in all four shipped themes, kept out of what apps may use, and `look::metric` (the 4 px grid, the radii and the chrome's sizes). The pull requests after it build the one layout crate `deskgeom`, the paint order, the icon set, the window chrome, the pane, the taskbar and tray, the menu, the wheel, and the finish; the first change a person sees is the window chrome, and the founder tests the head of the taskbar, pane and menu stack together. This row supersedes only the placement half of `2026-11-14-panel-history`, which put the history on the desk under the windows: it moves into the pane, and that row's look rules and limits stay. `2026-11-14-desktop-lines-ask-on-the-card` is unchanged, and the row at decisions.md:229 is not edited. One consequence for colours: one that clears `window` but falls short on `hover` or `shell` is now refused, and the message names the pair (light `muted #666666` is 4.32:1 on `hover`, dark `text #888898` is 4.07:1). A theme file or an `appearance_set` with one is refused as before; a saved appearance from before the two tokens is not refused whole, but replayed without that one colour, keeping the theme, scale, text size, reduced motion and legible type, with a line in the audit log (`Appearance::decode_audited`, which the shell uses).
