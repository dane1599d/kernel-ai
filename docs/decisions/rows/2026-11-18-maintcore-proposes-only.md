decision: The `maint` agent's rules are a host crate, `maintcore`, that proposes and applies nothing: a crash loop (3 restarts in 10 minutes) or a reported fault gives one line for the panel and the read `:fault <service>`, at most once an hour per service
---
ADR 0013 section 7 says `maint` reads health and proposes, and never applies an ask-tier change without the person. `maintcore` holds no handle and makes no call, so it has nothing it could apply. A proposal is a line and a read the person may type (`:fault <service>`). Any change the person then makes is their own line, through the one door, at its own tier. `Watch::observe` takes the supervisor's restarts and the faults services report. Three restarts inside a 10-minute window are a crash loop, and one proposal names it. The same service is not proposed again for an hour, so a loop cannot flood the panel. A fault proposes once per service per hour too. Names are held to a service name's shape (lower-case letters, digits, `-` and `_`, at most 32), so a name with a newline or a `:` cannot write a second command into the line. A clock that goes back counts as the last time seen, so the window never widens. At most 64 services are watched, and per service only the restarts inside the window are kept. The OS half (`user/src/bin/maint`, row X1) feeds it from SCHED_STATS, health and the supervisor's records, and posts the lines to the panel (G-core's turn, G3). Not wired into anything yet.
