needed-by: P4, built 2026-11-25 (bug-hunt-4 H4-16 and H4-17; before v0.1 is tagged)
decision: The slot disk's partitions are ADR 0008's fixed sizes, each slot's volume is its files' size, and the slot record carries the workspace version
---
The slot disk (`xtask/src/image/slots.rs`) sized `kai-r`, `kai-a` and `kai-b` to this build's files plus a quarter and 16 MiB, with `kai-data` right after `kai-b`, and `slot_image` sized an update the same way from its own files. So a machine installed from a release image refused nearly any later, larger release (`updated`'s `length > room`, on the update disk and online), and no update could grow the slots. Now the partitions are ADR 0008 section 1's: ESP 64 MiB, `kai-r` 256 MiB, `kai-a` and `kai-b` 1 GiB each, 2,368 MiB before `kai-data` (`PARTITION_MIB`). Each slot's FAT32 volume is its files' size in whole MiB (`fat32::fitting_sectors`, never under FAT32's 33 MiB floor) at the start of its partition, the rest zeros, and `slot_image` is the same size, so a download is the image's size and an install's slot is what an update writes there; the ESP's volume fills its 64 MiB. A release disk whose files do not fit a partition is refused by name. A boot case's disk grows a partition that is too small instead, since a test build is larger than a release (about 219 MiB of slot files in CI on 2026-11-25, against the release's 113 MiB) and is never installed; it prints a line when it does. The disks are written a piece at a time and sparse, never held whole in memory: the slot disk is 2,370 MiB (it was about 940 MiB, all of it written) and the release image 10,562 MiB raw (it was 8,729 MiB), which compresses to about what it did, since the new space is zeros. `build_keeping_b` copies slot B's whole partition from the old disk into a new one, a MiB at a time, skipping zeros. `updated`'s `step=check` (the `update-stage` case) copies the running slot's image, its FAT32 volume's own size, not the whole partition, which would now be 1 GiB under TCG. The `kai-slot 1` record's version is now `[workspace.package]`'s (`notices::workspace_version`), not `0.0.0`: `kernel/Cargo.toml` only inherits it. Its channel stays `dev` until release-image learns the tag. **Limit:** release images built before this change (v0.1.0-rc.1 among them) have content-sized slots that no update can grow; only a reinstall from a new image, or P8's repartitioning migration, gives them the ADR's sizes.
