The budgets are for a runner of baseline speed, and the case already measures how slow its runner is. Under KVM, gated, a figure is now a miss only when it reaches its budget times the reference median over the 16 ms baseline, never less than one and never over the 3.5 slow factor (`kabi::perf::misses_scaled`). Before a scene's second run the reference is timed again, and both runs are judged by the larger of the two medians (`judging_reference`), so a runner that slows within the boot is seen; `desktop` still writes its `OVER BUDGET` lines against the bare budgets, and the case logs each one the scaling lets pass. The three failures it must pass (figure, p99, reference ratio, scaled budget): #1119's run 45116384449, (f) 20.72 ms at 1.34x, 22.40 ms; #1141's run 45115320598, (g) 9.26 ms at 2.52x, 20.17 ms; batch #1261's run 45119588797, (g) 13.65 ms at 1.85x, 14.80 ms. What it still catches: on main's usual runner (run 45109962162, 1.29x) the (g) budget is 10.3 ms against a measured 6.96 ms, so a regression of about half again, in both runs, fails; at or under the baseline every budget is as before. The reference tracks the composite only roughly (#1141's runner was 2.52x on the reference but (g) only 1.2 to 1.3 times main's; #1261's was 1.85x and (g) about 2 times), and #1261 and #1119 pass with only about 8% headroom while #1261's own (a) moved 36% between its two runs. So every gated figure is also logged as `desk-perf: ratio: ...` with its value, the ratio and the value over the ratio. The plan: after about two weeks of main runs (to 2026-12-07), those lines decide whether the scale is capped (near 2x) or damped (only part of the ratio), or, if runners like #1261's still fail with no regression, gets a floor above one; a constant budget is not widened. Separately, (f)'s best-of-two rerun died with `cannot start hog-3: Busy`: a killed hog holds its name until it is reaped, and the rerun started 10 ms after the kill. `stop_hogs` now waits, up to 10 s, until each killed hog is out of the process table, and fails the case with `the killed hogs were not reaped` if one is not; on a TCG or ungated run, where there is no second run, it logs that and goes on.
