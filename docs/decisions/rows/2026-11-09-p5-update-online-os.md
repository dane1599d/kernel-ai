decision: `updated` streams the next release into the idle slot over TLS on a `NetDest` of its own, and takes the update snapshot after the checks and before the slot can boot.
---
The OS half of P5 task 10, on `updatenet` (#694) and the shared ring-3 client `user::tls` (#753). `step=online` runs the update disk's flow on the network: the root is the newer of the slot's own and `/system/trust`'s, `kaituf` refreshes the metadata from the repository's Pages host, the release must be newer than every release this machine has committed and fit the idle slot (in slot R, `into=A` or `into=B` names it: `:reinstall`), and the image comes in 2 MiB chunks by `Range` through GitHub's redirect to its release-assets host, each checked against the signed chunk list before it is written. A record of the last chunk checked in `/system/updates` lets a later run resume. `updated` moves to the hard-float programs, as `pkg` did. Two changes in `updatenet`: `stage` takes a `recheck` that asks the signed metadata again once the image has checked out (a long download can span a newer release or an expired timestamp; C's review of #694), and `Boot::before_trial` runs after the checks and before `stage_end`, so the update snapshot of the system volume (ADR 0008 §10) is taken while the slot still cannot boot, never between the trial and the restart. The cases serve this build as release 2 from the HTTPS mock, signed by the run's test root: an update and its refusal by anti-rollback after the reboot, a flipped byte (its chunk is refused and nothing of it is written, the slot stays Updating), and an expired timestamp (refused by name). As for `pkg`, the plan line says which repository (`online=test`, the mock's three hosts on port 443, its test CA the bundle's `netroots/1.der`), and the shipping system's waits for the follow-up that gives `updated` a request for it. The mock's CA is now one per xtask run, so an image can carry it before its mock starts. TLS is linked into `updated` itself, as into `pkg`, and not routed through T's `fetch` (the manager, 2026-11-09; the `p5-pkg-online-os` row has the reasons). The memory cost, on debug builds: `updated`'s loaded segments went from 1.22 MiB (soft-float) to 2.53 MiB (hard-float with rustls and the crypto), +1.31 MiB, resident in every desktop boot; the 520 MiB floor `DESK_TLS_MIB` of `desk-agent` and `desk-takeover` covers it with `pkg`'s. Speed: `update-online` prints the MB/s of the image's download (bytes fetched over the whole stage: the chunks, their checks, the writes and the read-back). `kainet` sends `Connection: close` on every request (`kainet/src/http/head.rs`), so a chunk of 2 MiB is a TLS connection of its own and a 2.5 GB image would be about 1,250 handshakes; keep-alive in `kainet` is the lever, and the figure from `full-ci` is in the pull request.
