needed-by: Agents, built 2026-11-18 (lane AG; ADR 0013 section 5; the follow-up named in row 2026-11-11-goalcore-end-in-flight-counts; host only)
decision: A step that a goal's end cuts off while it runs keeps the `actlog` entry the door made for it: its late finish is taken once, after the end, so `undo goals` and cancel's offer find everything the goal did
---
Before this, when a goal was stopped or gave up while a step ran, `Goal::end` closed the step as failed, and the runner's later `finish_step` was answered `NoStepRunning`. So the `actlog` entry that the door had already written for that step was never recorded in the goal. `undo goals` and `goal_cancel`'s offer to undo every step could not find it, though the change was made. Now `end` marks the step with its own note, `ENDED_IN_FLIGHT`. `finish_step` with no step in flight takes that one step's late report once. That works only on an ended goal whose last step is failed with that note and no entry. The step keeps the entry and the tokens the report gives, and how it came out: a step the door says ran becomes `ran`, with the counts moved from failed to ok. Its note becomes `FINISHED_AFTER_END`, so a second report finds no step. A door failure whose words are exactly the mark is prefixed, so the mark is only ever the end's. `kai-goals 1` does not change: what it holds still reads back, with its counts equal to its log's. `goalcore/tests/props.rs` now keeps the entry the door made for every step that touched the system, on time or late, and checks after every step that each step's entry is the one the door made (none missing, none invented) and that `undo_entries` is exactly those. It also counts the late finishes it reached. With the late finish taken out, both props tests fail.
