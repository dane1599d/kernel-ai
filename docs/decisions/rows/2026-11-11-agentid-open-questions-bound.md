decision: `agentid` counts every open question against the most open at once, `kai`'s included, when an agent other than `kai` asks; random sequences of installs, upgrades, retires, calls, questions and answers are held to a model of the roster after every step
---
`Asks::ask` held `kai` to `MAX_OPEN` (16) questions in all and any other agent to `MAX_PER_AGENT` (4) of its own and to the `MAX_OPEN - KAI_ROOM` (12) places that `kai` does not hold back, but never counted the total for an agent other than `kai`. With `kai` holding 16 questions open (typed lines that ask), an agent could still open 12 more, so 28 waited where the constant and the error's own words ("`MAX_OPEN` questions wait") say 16. The bound on what can keep the person from being asked held (28, not unbounded); the stated number did not. `ask` now also answers `Full` when `MAX_OPEN` questions wait, whoever asks, after the per-agent check as before. A test fills the 16 places with `kai`'s questions and has an agent refused, then admitted when one is answered, then refused again at 16.

`agentid/tests/props.rs` is how it was found: a model of the roster (who is live, under which badge and name, with which grants) and of the open questions is kept beside the real ones, and 300 seeds of 220 random steps each (install, upgrade, retire, make a caller from a badge, admit a call, ask, answer by a person or by an agent's mark, spend an answer on the right or a tampered call, withdraw, consent words, hostile name text) check every answer against the model and, after every step, that a badge is on the roster once and never handed out again, a name is on it once, the four built-ins keep badges 1 to 4, a caller is live exactly while its agent is (and never on another roster), questions are within their bounds, an agent's mark is never a person's, and a yes is spent only on the agent, row, entry and words it was for. Nothing else it checked was wrong.
