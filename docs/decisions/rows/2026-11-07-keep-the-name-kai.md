decision: The assistant keeps the name "Kai" after a first-pass trademark check found registered KAI marks for AI assistants.
---
The free first-pass check on 2026-11-06 searched trademark aggregator sites, not the USPTO, EUIPO or WIPO databases themselves, and was not legal advice. "Kai" overlaps registered KAI marks for conversational AI software: Kasisto, Inc. (US 5549199 and 5549201, class 42, 2018; Canada TMA1086716; a pending 2024 application, serial 98605261, for conversational digital assistants) and KAI Conversations Ltd (US 8190697, classes 9 and 42). No exact "Kernel AI" mark was found; HI LLC holds KERNEL marks (one in computer and software services, 6091561), and an iOS app named KernelAI is an offline AI assistant. Offered a rename of the user-facing name before the tag, the founder chose "Keep Kai" on 2026-11-07, accepting the risk of a later objection. Internal names (kaifs, kai-cache and the rest) were never in question.
