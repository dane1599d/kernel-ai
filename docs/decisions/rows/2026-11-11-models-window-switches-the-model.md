decision: The Models window switches the model on a person's key, through Kai's `model_use` row, as Settings changes a setting
---
**The interface.** `kai:models` is 0.1.1: `models.use-model(sha256) -> u32` (64 lowercase hex digits, else the call traps) and `listing.on-used(ticket, result<string, string>)`, each `@since(version = 0.1.1)`, so `wit-check` holds the frozen 0.1.0. `wasmhost` queues a switch as a `SettingsRequest` of `Kind::Change`, `use <sha256>`, carrying `user`, true only during `ui_input` or `ui_events_user`; a call may make one, at most 4 wait, and a call that faults takes its switch back. **The way.** comphost's Settings transport carries it on a second `Channel` (`Channel::Models`, only for the app the image grants `kai:models`): the same `settings_ledger`, which answers a switch no key or click made with why not at once, then `models put` to `desktop` (`deskcore::models_wire`, the Settings wire's `Words` with `models` words and `models-window` as the one caller, in a queue of its own, so neither app can put the other's requests), and the shell's `take_changes` hands it to `Shell::models_change`. That checks the app and `user` again and runs `model_use` with consent `user-click:models`, logged with `Inverse::Model`, so `undo` puts the model before back. The agent pressing Use through `ui_act` is never a switch. **Import** stays Kai's or the console's (`models import`): it has no undo, so it asks, and an Allow / Deny card opens only as the reply to a terminal line, which a window cannot send; the window's import choice keeps its note. The manager chose this (option 1): a no-undo action never runs on a window click alone. Once the shell can open the trusted card itself (lane C's install-card follow-up 2), an Import click may raise that card (a TODO in the window marks it). **The window.** A read-only window with `View::switches`: Use says "Use from the next restart" and is bound to no row; the note says it is switching, then what the shell answered, and the list is read again. **The case.** `ui-models-use`: fixture-2 is imported with a typed `models import` and the trusted path's Allow, then a click on the window's title bar and the harness's keys (`tab ret` for Refresh, `tab down down tab ret` for Use, pinned by `apps/models-ui/tests/keys.rs`) switch to it; the log shows `consent=user-click:models`, and `undo` switches back. #556.
