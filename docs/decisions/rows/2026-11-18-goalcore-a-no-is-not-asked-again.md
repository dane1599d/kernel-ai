decision: A step of a goal that the person said no to is not asked a second time: when the planner writes it again the goal pauses, and only the person's resume lets it be asked once more
---
After a no, a goal goes on and its planner is asked for the next step. A small model given the same goal and the same history writes the same step, so the card came back each turn until the budget ran out, with nobody but the person to stop it. Now `goalcore::runner::Runner` keeps, for each goal, the steps the person said no to, whichever way they said it: the card's no (`Outcome::Denied`) and the no to a step goalcore itself held (`Goal::deny`). A step with the same row, the same paths and the same call as one of them is not proposed: it is not logged, not sent to the door and not counted as asked. The goal is paused as the person's own pause is (`Pause::User`), the runner says `Event::Repeated`, and what it held against the goal is dropped. So every card for a refused step has an act of the person between it and the last one: a resume, after which a second no pauses the goal at the next repeat in the same way. What the step was expected to cost is not part of what was refused; another path or another call is another step and is asked as usual. The list is the runner's memory, not the goal's file: each denied step has a log record, so it is no longer than the log, it goes when the goal ends, and after a restart of the agent a refused step may be asked one more time. Pausing was chosen over a question to the person because `goal_resume` exists today and the queue has no way to answer a question yet.
