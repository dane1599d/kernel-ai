needed-by: Agents, built 2026-11-09 (lane E; the manager asked for a net budget in `kaiagent` and an end-to-end test; host crates only, no OS wiring before the tag)
decision: A manifest's `net` line may say the bytes a day its network may move (`bytes/day=Nm`, 10 MiB by default, 512 MiB at most), counted by a pure `DayBytes` counter
---
ADR 0013 section 6 bounds an agent's tokens, KV positions and pool share (the `model` line) but not what its network may move, and an agent that can reach a host could move as much as the link allows. The `net` line now takes a budget after the destination: `net proto:tcp,ports:443,hosts:api.weather.example bytes/day=10m`.

- **Grammar.** `bytes/day=Nm`, N whole MiB above zero and at most 512, the same shape as `pool=64m` and refused the same ways (`BudgetKey`, `BudgetUnit`, `BudgetNumber`, `BudgetZero`, `BudgetOver`). A destination that says nothing takes the default, 10 MiB (est.: a few hundred fetches of an API answer or a page); `net none` moves nothing and takes no budget (`none bytes/day=1m` is refused). `Manifest::format` always writes it after a destination, so what is written reads back as the same manifest. `Manifest` gains `net_budget`; `net` is unchanged.
- **The ceiling, 512 MiB a day (est.),** is well past what an agent that reads pages and calls APIs needs. A request for more is a download and not a budget, and P5's verified downloads are for that.
- **The counter.** `kaiagent::DayBytes` is pure: no clock, the caller says the day (the host's calendar day, as `agentsched`'s `Now.day`). A later day starts again at zero; an earlier day counts as the last one, so setting the clock back does not give the budget back. A charge that would pass the limit is refused whole and counts nothing (a transfer stops at the byte it cannot pay for), a charge of zero is always paid, a sum that would overflow is refused and never wraps, and the limit can change with what was used staying. The network service will charge it after the tag; nothing in the OS uses it yet.
- **The end-to-end test** (`agentid/tests/end_to_end.rs`, with `agentsched` and `goalcore` as dev-dependencies): a manifest becomes an agent with a badge; its grants are the door's ceiling (`fs_trash` is `OutsideGrants`); the person's words go to `kai` and not to it; the scheduler runs its planning requests under its 600 tokens a day with the person's request first and refuses the next one over quota until the day turns; its goal runs two steps through the door stamped `agent=report-bot`; a step outside the goal's folders asks, the agent cannot answer for the person (`NotThePerson`), and the person's yes runs that one step.

No WIT, no package kind, no `actlog` and no OS crate changes; `kaiagent`, `agentid` and their dev-dependencies are host crates.
